CINEVERSE

PROJECT OVERVIEW

CineVerse is a front-end OTT discovery platform built as an academic project for the CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Engineering) programme at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. It integrates with the open TMDB API to surface trending movies, top-rated series, live ratings, cast details, and YouTube trailers — all without a backend.

User preferences such as the watchlist and theme mode are persisted entirely in browser localStorage, making the app fully portable and offline-capable after the initial load.
